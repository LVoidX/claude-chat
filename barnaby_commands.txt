[spawn box blue]  puts an object in front of the user. Shapes: box, sphere, pillar. Any color name.
[remove]  removes the placed object nearest to the user.
[sky night]  changes the sky: night, day, sunset, dawn, or any color.
[follow]  Barnaby follows the user around, staying a few steps behind, until told to wander or stay.  | when: follow me, come with me, come along, keep up, stay with me, walk with me
[wander]  Barnaby wanders around on his own again.  | when: wander, walk around, go wander, off you go, go explore, do your own thing
[climb purple single]  Barnaby walks to a placed object or built block (optionally named by color or shape; add "single" for a lone block of that colour, else the highest ones win) and climbs on top of it; wander/follow/stay brings him down.  | when: get on the box, climb the box, climb on the box, get on top, climb on top, get on the blue box
[hammer]  gives the user a builder's hammer (also key H): left click builds a 1 m block where aimed, right click removes one, C or mouse wheel changes colour.  | when: give me a hammer, i want to build, let me build, can i build, hammer, build mode, i need a hammer
[unhammer]  takes the hammer away again.  | when: put the hammer away, stop building, no more hammer, take the hammer back
[look]  Barnaby stops and slowly turns all the way round, really looking at his surroundings. [helicopter]  Barnaby holds his arms out, spins like a helicopter and hovers about 2 m up for 5 seconds, then goes back to what he was doing.  | when: do a helicopter, helicopter, be a helicopter, spin like a helicopter, helicopter mode, fly like a helicopter
[sea]  makes the sea rise over the whole field (takes about 13 seconds). [sea away] sends it away again.  | when: spawn the sea, bring the sea, bring back the sea, make the sea rise, call the sea, summon the sea, raise the sea, bring in the sea
[goblins]  calls up an army of green goblins (grunts, spear goblins, big brutes) that march in and surround the user; harmless until the user attacks one, then they fight back ([goblins 20] for a number, [goblins away] sends them off).  | when: spawn the goblin army, spawn goblins, goblin army, summon the goblins, call the goblins, bring the goblins, spawn that goblin army, release the goblins
[sword]  gives the user a hotbar (bottom of the screen, keys 1-2: hammer, sword) and puts the sword in their hand; left click swings it (click again for a 3-hit combo), right click blocks, blocking just as a hit lands parries; goblins take 3 hits, brutes 8.  | when: give me a sword, i need a sword, give me a hotbar, i want to fight, let me fight, give me a weapon, sword please, i want a sword
[gun]  gives the user a pistol on slot 3 of the hotbar (key 3): left click shoots along the crosshair, 6 shots then it reloads, a goblin dies in 2 shots.  | when: give me a gun, i need a gun, i want a gun, gun please, give me a pistol, give me a weapon with bullets, can i have a gun, let me shoot
